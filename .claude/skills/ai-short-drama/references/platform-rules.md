# Platform rules and copyright (read 2026-10-22)

Rules change often — open the official page again before publishing. Not legal advice.

## China — 《人工智能生成合成内容标识办法》 (in force 2025-09-01)
- Issued by CAC, MIIT, MPS and NRTA. Two kinds of label: **explicit** (text, sound or graphic the viewer can see/hear) and **implicit** (metadata in the file).
- Deleting, altering, forging or hiding AI labels is prohibited.
- Sources: https://www.xinhuanet.com/tech/20250909/fb164c6d092146aa8e13ddc283fe416a/c.html , https://www.cls.cn/detail/2133981

## Douyin (抖音)
- When posting, declare the work as AI-generated in the publish settings; content made with third-party AIGC tools must be declared by the user.
- Undeclared content the platform suspects is AI gets a "疑似使用AI生成技术" label; don't remove platform labels.
- AIGC is allowed; fake, infringing or vulgar traffic-bait content is not.
- Sources are news reports (财联社, IT之家, China Law Translate) — Douyin's own rule text was not read (**部分未验证**). Check 95152.douyin.com before publishing.
- Micro-dramas in China: since 2024 tiered review; series without review/filing may not be distributed online (news reports, exact rules 未逐字核对). Matters if the user publishes a paid series in mainland China.

## TikTok
- Community Guidelines, Integrity and Authenticity (page shows effective 2026-09-24): AI-generated or significantly edited media showing **realistic scenes or people** must be labelled; unlabelled content may be removed, restricted or labelled by TikTok.
- A label doesn't make misleading or harmful content allowed.
- Unoriginal or reused content with nothing new added is not eligible for the For You feed.
- Source: https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/integrity-authenticity

## YouTube / YouTube Shorts
- In Studio, answer **"AI use" = Yes** for realistic altered or synthetic content: real people saying/doing things they didn't, altered real events or places, realistic scenes that never happened, AI music as the main focus.
- Not required: clearly unrealistic or animated content, beauty/colour filters, captions, AI help with scripts, titles or thumbnails, cloning your own voice for voice-over.
- Animated/non-photoreal → label shows in the description; photoreal → may show on the player. YouTube may auto-label (its tools, C2PA metadata, detection).
- Repeated non-disclosure → forced label, removal or YPP suspension. Disclosure itself doesn't reduce reach or monetisation.
- The help page doesn't single out Shorts. Source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491
- Monetisation: from 2025-07-15 the YPP policy targets **mass-produced, repetitive ("inauthentic") content** — demonetised, not banned (news: https://www.plagiarismtoday.com/2025/07/08/youtube-targets-inauthentic-content/ ).

**Rule of thumb for this user:** an AI drama with realistic-looking people → declare AI on every platform. A cartoon/anime 漫剧 → YouTube may not require it, but Douyin (Chinese law) and TikTok still expect the AI declaration; declaring is the safe default.

## Copyright of the result
- US Copyright Office, AI report Part 2 (2025-01-29): prompts alone don't make you the author; human-authored parts (script, selection, arrangement, edits) can be protected, case by case. https://copyright.gov/newsnet/2025/1060.html
- China: Beijing Internet Court's 2023 AI-image case recognised copyright where the user made substantial creative choices (public reports; judgment text not checked here, 未验证 details).
- Practical: the script, storyboard, edit and voice direction are what the user can own. **Adapting someone else's novel needs the rights holder's permission** — the biggest risk in "小说转短剧".
- Model licences also bind use (see tools-compared.md): FLUX.1 dev non-commercial, HunyuanVideo region exclusions, 火宝短剧 software non-commercial.
- Voice: cloning a real person's voice or using a real person's face needs their consent; never imitate celebrities.
