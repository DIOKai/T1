# Millwork, joinery and FF&E

Read the adopted standard's joinery and furniture/fittings/equipment rules, with relevant openings, glazing and decoration operations. Classify by supplied scope and the complete source table; do not assume all cabinetry is measured under one section.

Read item schedules, location plans, shop/design details and specifications. Record each type, overall dimensions, multiplicity, material/veneer/grade, matching requirements, substrate, finish, fabrication/jointing, fixing background, hardware, countertop, lighting and service components. Retain stable item IDs even when several items share the same description. Reconcile item counts and repeated rooms against locations and schedules.

Check whether the price is for a complete assembly or components. Establish supply, fabrication, delivery, installation, fixings, finishing, shop drawings, samples and mock-ups from the actual quote and relevant measurement rule. Avoid pricing component hardware, integral glazing or finishes twice. Mark unsupported design details and provisional assemblies explicitly.

For loose/proprietary equipment, retain manufacturer/model and performance requirements when supplied; substitution requires equivalent function and client/design acceptance where applicable. Check delivery dimensions/access, storage, installation sequencing, structural backing, utilities, final connections, commissioning, warranties and owner/GC/vendor responsibility. Reconcile service connections and associated work against package boundaries and the selected source's inclusion notes.

A procurement list can use purchase units while installation measurement uses another supported basis. Keep the crosswalk and conversion visible. Do not infer market prices or company codes from model numbers, item suffixes or similar descriptions.
