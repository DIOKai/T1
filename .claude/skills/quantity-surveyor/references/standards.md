# Standards selection and applicability

Public status checked against the official sources below on **1 October 2026**. Recheck current editions, laws, forms and licences when a decision depends on them. This is original selection guidance; it does not reproduce standards or establish clause-level compliance.

## Select four separate bases

Identify jurisdiction (including local authority), project use, appointment, contract and professional status. Establish separately: detailed work measurement, cost classification/reporting, area definition and lifecycle analysis. Record adopted name/edition, amendments, authority, exceptions and actual clause evidence. Apply applicable law/regulatory conditions, professional obligations and agreed contract requirements; identify any conflict for the project team rather than silently choosing a method. When the appointment is silent, recommend and agree a suitable convention. A contract may retain an older edition; disclose the difference from current practice.

## Regional candidates and specific obligations

These examples help select evidence, not prescribe universal local requirements:

- Australia/New Zealand: ANZSMM 2022 is offered as a common building-work measurement basis. Check actual adoption, amendments and NZ adaptations rather than assuming migration in every contract. [NZIQS publication](https://nziqs.co.nz/NZIQS/NZIQS/Shop/Item_Detail.aspx?iProductCode=ANZSMM2022), [AIQS publications](https://www.aiqs.com.au/publications).
- Hong Kong: HKIS lists HKSMM5 (fifth edition, 2025); ArchSD has a separate 2020 elemental measurement method. Choose the appointment's required basis and edition, not a presumed single method for all projects. [HKIS catalogue](https://www.hkis.org.hk/tc/publication_sales.html), [ArchSD](https://www.archsd.gov.hk/en/publications-publicity/standard-method-of-measurement-for-building-elements.html).
- United States: owner's specifications may use elemental or work-result classifications. GSA P120 is agency-specific and specifies ASTM UNIFORMAT II; its requirements are not automatically applicable to a private construction project. [GSA active P120](https://www.gsa.gov/directives-library/p120-public-buildings-service-cost-and-schedule-management-policy-1).
- NSW, Australia: an estimated development cost planning-submission report has a defined purpose/boundary and qualification requirements, distinct from a full owner budget. Verify project threshold/category and the current state form/AIQS requirements. Do not imply the assistant can provide required professional sign-off. [NSW EDC requirements](https://www.planning.nsw.gov.au/the-planning-system/estimated-development-cost), [AIQS second edition notice](https://www.aiqs.com.au/standards/construction-cost-assessments-for-nsw-estimated-development-cost-reports-2nd-edition).

For any location, verify relevant taxes, permits, building/fire/accessibility/environmental requirements, procurement rules and qualification/signature requirements through the actual authorities when they affect scope/cost or advice. Avoid inventing a worldwide legal inventory. A technical building requirement and a quantity-measurement convention answer different questions.

For new freestanding construction, include utility capacity, connection/diversion requirements and utility-provider or authority approvals in that verification scope; a building permit discussion alone does not establish those interfaces.

## Rights and reference mechanism

No publisher files, spreadsheets, extracted databases, exhaustive classifications or rate libraries are bundled. Package checklists/templates are original. [RICS publication copyright notices](https://www.rics.org/content/dam/ricsglobal/documents/standards/NRM1_Oct2022.pdf) reserve reproduction rights; its supporting-template customization statements are subject to terms and do not establish skill redistribution permission. [RICS website terms](https://www.rics.org/footer/rics-org-terms-and-conditions) also address automated extraction/AI-related use and links. No scraper or mirror is provided.

[ISO copyright terms](https://www.iso.org/copyright.html) protect publications and require permission for relevant uses. The current [CSI/TCS EULA](https://theconstructionstandard.com/eula), restriction 2(9), includes AI augmentation restrictions; an ordinary purchased licence is not sufficient evidence of that permission. Legacy licences need their own review. [CSI licence FAQ](https://www.csiresources.org/standards/cds-faq).

ICMS/IPMS publications permit conditional unchanged copies, not unrestricted derivative extraction. This package uses citations and original guidance only. Rights to local/commercial libraries remain unverified. Follow [evidence-and-interfaces.md](evidence-and-interfaces.md) for a user-supplied reference register, applicable permissions and unavailable-rule fallbacks. No publisher endorsement, licence to third-party standards or certification is asserted.
