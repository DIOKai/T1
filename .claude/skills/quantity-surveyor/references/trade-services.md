# Services packages and interfaces

Use this reference for mechanical, electrical, plumbing, fire/security/IT and transportation package reviews. Early estimates may use a supported functional/area/point basis; detailed measurement needs the applicable design evidence. These prompts are not engineering design instructions.

Use the adopted standard's drainage, mechanical, electrical, transportation and related builders' work sections. For early elemental planning, use the selected cost-planning source and supported pricing/uncertainty evidence. Read complete units, descriptions, inclusions and adjacent notes before measuring.

Establish layouts, schematics, sections, plantroom/details, equipment schedules and specified capacities/performance. Record the quantity basis for each rate: equipment count, point count with its included assembly, route length with fittings convention, served/treated area, capacity or whole system. Define served area and exclusions explicitly; it is not automatically project floor area. Do not compare unlike quantity bases or use a benchmark as a measured trade rate.

Check plant/equipment versus distribution, terminal devices, insulation, supports, fittings, controls/BMS, electrical power, drainage, fire stopping, access panels, penetrations, plinths and builder's work. The source distinguishes detailed fittings where information exists from inclusive measurement where it does not; preserve that convention and reconcile quotation inclusions before adding fittings separately.

Create a small responsibility matrix when interfaces span trades: scope, quantity basis, supplying party, installing party, connection/testing owner, included price, query. In particular check electrical power to mechanical equipment, controls wiring, fire/security/IT links, landlord tie-ins and owner equipment. Mark ownership conflicts rather than allocating them twice.

Inspect testing, balancing, commissioning, certification, as-built information, manuals, training, spare parts and maintenance obligations required by the brief/quote. Review programme, procurement lead times, restricted hours, shutdowns and existing conditions for evidenced cost consequences. Seek specialist clarification for missing capacity/performance or design compliance; continue supported quantities without inventing engineering or productivity assumptions.
