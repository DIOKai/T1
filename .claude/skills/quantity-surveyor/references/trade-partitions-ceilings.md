# Partitions, linings and ceilings

Read with the drawing workflow and the selected source's complete measurement table. The prompts below identify scope and information; they do not prescribe universal units, deduction thresholds or bill items.

Use the adopted standard's sections for proprietary partitions/linings, suspended ceilings, finishes, decoration and fire-related work. Read full units, descriptions, inclusions and adjacent notes. For NRM work, consult the authorised NRM 2 work-section tables; other project conventions govern where selected.

Establish plans/elevations and reflected ceiling plans, structural and finished heights, partition types, framing and lining build-ups, thicknesses/layers, acoustic/fire performance, fixing backgrounds, curvature, joints, trims and finishes. Record which specification defines each assembly. Count openings, access panels and integral fittings with location references; inspect ceiling void services and support interfaces.

Check whether each quoted system includes framing, normal joint treatment, perimeter work, additional support, cutting/openings and fitting around services. Distinguish proprietary system finishes from later work by another trade, and secondary ceiling support from the ceiling system. Use the full relevant rule and actual quotation before deciding what is included or separately measured.

Check doors/frames/hardware, glazing, bulkheads, pelmets, fire/acoustic seals, backing for heavy fixtures, hangers, structural supports and service coordination for omitted or duplicated ownership. A separately listed component is not automatically a separately chargeable item. Split supply/installation or owner/GC scope only when the project procurement boundary supports it.

Keep differing systems, heights, ratings, finishes and locations distinguishable where they affect measurement or price. Query unsupported heights or build-ups instead of manufacturing quantities. Reconcile partition plans to room elevations and ceiling areas to reflected ceiling plans, retaining the selected deduction convention.
