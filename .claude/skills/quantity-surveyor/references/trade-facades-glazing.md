# Facades, shopfronts and glazing

Read the adopted standard's cladding/curtain-wall, windows/screens/shopfront and glazing rules, plus applicable supports, waterproofing, doors and metalwork. Select the complete system or separate operation using project specifications and source rules.

Inspect elevations, plans, sections, glazing schedules, system/fabrication details and structural interfaces. Establish system type, panel sizes, glass build-up/performance, frame profiles/finish, opaque infills, insulation/membranes, anchors/brackets, movement joints, sealants, interfaces and support structure. Distinguish panel area, glazed area, opening counts and linear interfaces; do not mix area bases in rates.

Check the complete-system inclusions before measuring integral framing, glazing, insulation, fixings, hardware or operating equipment again. Distinguish glazing supplied with a component from glazing into prepared openings and identify the ownership of secondary structural support; confirm actual ownership and quoted scope rather than inventing a percentage allowance.

Reconcile integral doors/opening sashes, perimeter flashings, waterproofing, smoke/fire interfaces, adjoining construction and landlord works. Check specialist engineering, surveys, shop drawings, samples/mock-ups, approvals, access/lifting, protection, testing and commissioning where required. Keep design/engineering responsibility with the appropriate project party.

Calibrate each elevation/detail independently, reconcile repeated bays and counts, and inspect corner/return geometry. Record source-backed deductions and separate unsupported panel thicknesses, structural requirements or test allowances as queries. Value-engineering savings must include consequential support, installation, testing and maintenance changes.
