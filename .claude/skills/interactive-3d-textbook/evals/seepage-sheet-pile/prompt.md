I love the style of 'Every Joule' (iamtechartist.com/every-joule/) — an interactive 3D textbook: an open book on a desk, each diagram lifts off the page as black-ink line art, the important quantity glows in colour, and the lesson is predict → test → prove with a flap to lift for the explanation and a stamp when you're right. Make ONE lesson in that style for soil mechanics: 'Seepage under a sheet pile' — water flows from the high side to the low side under a sheet pile wall; the student predicts where the exit gradient is highest / whether piping (boiling) happens, changes the head difference and the pile depth with sliders, and sees the flow net (flow lines + equipotentials) and the exit gradient update. Use Three.js r170 (WebGL) and pmndrs postprocessing. It must look premium and also work on a phone.
