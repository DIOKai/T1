The answer states that the livery texture name inside the ytd must exactly match the texture name the shader references (for example <prefix>_sign_1, _sign_2), and that switching between two texture liveries needs FLAG_HAS_LIVERY in vehicles.meta (or names the modkit VMT_LIVERY_MOD livery-yft method as the alternative).
