The answer explains that the livery is drawn by a second diffuse texture (DiffuseSampler2) of the vehicle paint shaders that have a livery layer (vehicle_paint3/4 and similar), read through UV channel 1 (UVMap 1 / TexCoord1), or otherwise states clearly that the livery uses the second UV map rather than the normal body UVs.
